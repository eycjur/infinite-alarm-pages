Privacy Policy

Infinite Alarm ("the app") respects your privacy.

Data Collection

The app does not collect any data, including personal information.

  • Alarm settings (time, interval, count, labels, etc.) are stored only on your device.
  • Imported custom sound files are stored only on your device.
  • No data is ever sent to external servers.
  • No analytics tools or advertising SDKs are used.

Alarm Permission

The app requests permission to use the iOS alarm feature (AlarmKit) in order to ring alarms at the times you set. This permission is used solely for ringing alarms.

Location

If you use the location alarm feature, the app requests location permission (While Using the App). Location data is used only to determine, on your device, whether you have approached the place you chose.

  • Location is collected only while a location alarm is tracking (up to one hour after it starts), including in the background.
  • Location data is processed only on your device; it is never stored or sent anywhere. The coordinates of the place you chose are stored only on your device.
  • Map display and place search use Apple's map services (MapKit). Your search terms, and the coordinates of the point you choose on the map (to look up its name or address), are sent to Apple.

Notifications

The app requests notification permission to let you know when a location alarm has stopped tracking automatically. Only local notifications scheduled on your device are used; no external servers are involved.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us via GitHub Issues.
