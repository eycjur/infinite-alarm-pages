Frequently Asked Questions

Q. My alarms don't ring
A. Open the Settings app → Apps → Infinite Alarm and make sure alarm access is allowed. If a warning appears in the alarm list, you can open Settings directly from there.
Q. Do alarms ring in silent mode?
A. Yes. The app uses AlarmKit — the same mechanism as the built-in Clock app — so alarms ring even in silent mode and during Focus.
Q. How do I change the alarm volume?
A. Alarms follow the system alarm volume. On iOS 26 this is shared with the ringtone and notification volume in Settings → Sounds & Haptics; on iOS 27 and later you can set it separately under "Alarms and Timers". Note that the in-app preview button plays at the media volume, which may differ from the actual alarm volume.
Q. Can I use vibration only, without sound?
A. Yes. Choose "Vibration Only" in the Sound section of the alarm editor.
